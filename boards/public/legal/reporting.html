Reporting something

Last updated 14 September 2026

There is a Report this board item in the menu of every board. Use it if you can get to the board. If you cannot, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and say which board and what is wrong.

Tell me where it is and what it is. A link to the board and a sentence about the item is enough. You do not need to be the person affected.

What I act on

Sexual content involving a child. Removed as soon as I see the report, preserved as the law requires, and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children. The board goes, and so does whoever put it there. There is no appeal and no second chance. If you have found this anywhere online you can also report it directly at report.cybertip.org.

Intimate images of a real person, posted without their consent. This includes faked and AI-generated images of a real person. If you are the person in the image, or someone acting for them, say so — I will take it down within 48 hours of a valid request and remove any copies I can find. Include enough for me to identify the item, and a statement that the image is of you and you did not consent to it being posted.

Credible threats of violence against a person or a group. Removed, and passed to law enforcement where it looks warranted.

Copyright. Its own process, on the Copyright page. Send it to the agent named there, not through the report button, or it may not count as a valid notice.

Anything else that is against the law. Tell me what law and where it is, and I will look.

What I do not act on

I do not moderate boards for rudeness, arguments, bullying or harassment, and I do not arbitrate between people who know each other. This is not because those things do not matter. It is that one person cannot referee private walls fairly, and pretending otherwise would be worse than saying so plainly.

If somebody is being unpleasant on a board you own, you have faster tools than I am:

  • Take the item down yourself.
  • Revoke their key, in the keys panel. It stops working straight away.
  • Turn submissions off, in the submissions inbox.
  • Delete the board.

If somebody is being unpleasant on a board you do not own, ask whoever does. I will not make them take it down, and I will not tell you who they are.

I also do not settle arguments about who a board really belongs to, and I do not remove things for being embarrassing or unflattering.

What happens after you report

  1. The report reaches me by email straight away.
  2. I look at the board.
  3. I either act on it or I do not.
  4. If you left a way to reach you, I will tell you which.

I aim to look at everything within a few days, and at the first two categories above within hours. I am one person. There is no ticketing system and no appeal panel.

If you report things that are not true

The site records which key was held when a report was sent. Somebody using the report button to make trouble for a board they simply dislike will find their key revoked. Reporting in good faith and being wrong is fine — that is what reporting is for.

If your board was removed

Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and tell me why you think it was a mistake. I will read it and answer. Content in the first category above is never restored.
