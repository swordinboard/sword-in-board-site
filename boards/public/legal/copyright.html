Copyright

Last updated 14 September 2026

People put their own things on Pinhold. I do not check them. If something on this site is yours and should not be here, tell me and I will take it down.

Where to send a complaint

This is the registered agent for copyright notices under the DMCA:

[AGENT NAME] [YOUR LEGAL NAME or BUSINESS NAME] [POSTAL ADDRESS] [CITY, STATE, ZIP] United States [PHONE] [DMCA EMAIL]

What to put in it

For me to act on it, your notice has to include all six of these:

  1. Your signature — typing your full name in an email counts.
  2. What the work is. If there are several, list them.
  3. Where it is on Pinhold. A link to the board is best; the board's name and a description of the item will do.
  4. How to reach you: address, phone, email.
  5. This sentence: “I have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.”
  6. And this one: “The information in this notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.”

A notice missing these may not count as a valid one, and I may not be able to act on it.

Sending a false notice has costs. Under 17 U.S.C. §512(f), anyone who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing can be made to pay damages and legal fees. If you are unsure whether you hold the rights, ask a lawyer before sending.

What happens then

  1. I remove or hide the item as soon as I reasonably can.
  2. I tell the person who posted it what was removed and why.
  3. I pass them a copy of your notice, including the contact details in it.
  4. They can send a counter-notice.

If your item was removed and you think that was wrong

Send a counter-notice to the same address, containing:

  1. Your signature.
  2. What was removed and where it was.
  3. This sentence: “I swear, under penalty of perjury, that I have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.”
  4. Your name, address and phone number.
  5. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for the district where you live — or, if you live outside the US, of any district where I may be found — and that you will accept service of process from the person who complained.

I pass your counter-notice to them. If they do not file a court action within 10 to 14 business days, I may put the item back.

Repeat infringers

If the same person keeps posting other people's work, I close their boards and stop them making new ones. I decide when that point has been reached, and I am not obliged to give warnings first.
