Site rules

Last updated 15 September 2026

Pinhold is run by Sword in Board Workshop. Using it means you agree to what is on this page. If you do not agree, do not use it.

Treat every board as public

A board is protected by a passphrase and nothing more. Passphrases may be shared, forwarded, left on screens, and occasionally guessed. Anyone holding one can read the whole board associated with it, and the developer can read any board on the site.

So: put nothing on a board you would mind a stranger reading. Not passwords, not bank details, not medical or legal papers, not anything about another person that they would not want posted on a wall. The site is built to feel like a corkboard in a hallway, and that is exactly how private it is.

You are 13 or older

You need to be at least 13 to use Pinhold. If you are under 13, do not create a board or send a submission. Doing so will result in that content being removed.

The site asks you to confirm this the first time you use it on a device, and again the first time you open a board you have not been on before. It remembers that you did, so it is not a question you meet every visit unless you are switching between boards often.

What you may not put up

You may not use Pinhold to post, upload or link to:

  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18.
  • Intimate images of a real person shared without their consent, whether real or faked.
  • Real threats of violence, or anything urging someone to commit violence.
  • Work that is not yours to post without the owner's consent — other people's photos, writing, art or music.
  • Malware, phishing pages, or links to either. Links you write are shown as links inside an item; do not use that to send people somewhere harmful.
  • Automated scraping, bulk board creation, or attempts to guess passphrases.

Moderation

I am one person running a small site. I do not read boards looking for problems, and rely heavily on community reporting.

There is a Report this board item in the menu of every board. Use that if you can get to the board, or write to swordinboard@gmail.com if you cannot. Tell me which board it is and what is wrong with it. You do not need to be the person affected.

I will act on:

  • Sexual content involving a child. Always, immediately, and reported to the authorities.
  • Intimate images posted without the subject's consent.
  • Credible threats of violence.
  • Anything a court or law enforcement requires me to act on.

I can remove things and close boards

I can delete any item, any board, or any account, at any time, with or without warning, for any reason or none. In practice that means the things listed above, plus anything that is costing more to host than I am willing to pay. I am not obliged to keep a copy or to tell you first, but I will be as reasonable as possible.

Boards are deleted after 180 days

If nobody looks at a board for 180 days, it is deleted automatically — the board, its items and its pictures. Opening it resets the clock. Nothing warns you before this happens and there is no undo.

Pinhold is not a place to keep anything. It is a wall, not an archive. Keep your own copies of anything you would be sorry to lose.

Your content stays yours

You keep ownership of everything you put up. You give me permission to store it, copy it and show it to people who open the board — which is what running the site consists of, and nothing more. I will not sell it, license it to anyone, train anything on it or use it to advertise. That permission ends when you delete the content, except for backups that have not yet rolled over.

By putting something up you are confirming it is yours to put up, or that you have permission.

The site is free and comes with no promises

Pinhold is provided as it is. I do not promise it will be available, that it will keep your content, that it will be free of faults, or that it is fit for anything in particular. I may change it or shut it down at any time, but I really hope not to.

To the fullest extent the law allows, I am not liable for anything you lose by using it — including lost content, lost time, lost business, or anything someone else posts.

You are responsible for what you put up. If someone sues me because of something you posted, you agree to cover my costs in dealing with it.
