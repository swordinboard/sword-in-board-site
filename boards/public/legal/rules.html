Site rules

Treat every board as public

A board is protected by a passphrase and nothing more. Passphrases get shared, forwarded, left on screens and occasionally guessed. Anyone holding one can read the whole board, and I can read any board on the site.

So: put nothing on a board you would mind a stranger reading. Not passwords, not bank details, not medical or legal papers, not anything about another person that they would not want posted on a wall. The site is built to feel like a corkboard in a hallway, and that is exactly how private it is.

You are 13 or older

You need to be at least 13 to use Pinhold. If you are under 13, do not create a board or send a submission. If I find out an account belongs to someone under 13, I will delete it and anything on it.

What you may not put up

You may not use Pinhold to post, upload or link to:

  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18.
  • Intimate images of a real person shared without their consent, whether real or faked.
  • Real threats of violence, or anything urging someone to commit violence.
  • Work that is not yours to post — other people's photos, writing, art or music.
  • Anything else that is a crime where you are or where I am.
  • Malware, phishing pages, or links to either. Links you write are shown as links inside an item; do not use that to send people somewhere harmful.
  • Automated scraping, bulk board creation, or attempts to guess passphrases. The site counts failed attempts and will stop answering.

What I will and will not do about content

I am one person running a small site. I do not read boards looking for problems, and I have no moderation team.

I will act on:

  • Sexual content involving a child. Always, immediately, and reported to the authorities.
  • Intimate images posted without the subject's consent.
  • Credible threats of violence.
  • Valid copyright complaints — see Copyright.
  • Anything a court or law enforcement requires me to act on.

I will not generally act on:

  • Rudeness, arguments, bullying or harassment between people who know each other. If someone is being unpleasant on a board you own, you can take the item down, revoke their key, or delete the board. Those tools are yours, and they are faster than I am.
  • Being embarrassed by something true. If a board says something about you that you would rather it did not, ask whoever owns the board.
  • Disputes about who is the rightful owner of a board.

Reporting explains how to tell me about something and what happens next.

I can remove things and close boards

I can delete any item, any board, or any account, at any time, with or without warning, for any reason or none. In practice that means the things listed above, plus anything that is costing more to host than I am willing to pay. I am not obliged to keep a copy or to tell you first.

Boards are deleted after 180 days

If nobody looks at a board for 180 days, it is deleted automatically — the board, its items and its pictures. Opening it resets the clock. Nothing warns you before this happens and there is no undo.

Pinhold is not a place to keep anything. It is a wall, not an archive. Keep your own copies of anything you would be sorry to lose.

Your content stays yours

You keep ownership of everything you put up. You give me permission to store it, copy it and show it to people who open the board — which is what running the site consists of, and nothing more. I will not sell it, license it to anyone, train anything on it or use it to advertise. That permission ends when you delete the content, except for backups that have not yet rolled over.

By putting something up you are confirming it is yours to put up, or that you have permission.
